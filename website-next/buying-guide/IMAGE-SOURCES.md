# Buying guide product images: sources and terms

Collected 13 November 2026 for the website buying guide only. None of these go into the Lastbook app or library.

Each image is a local copy (no hotlinking) of the manufacturer's own product photo from the manufacturer's own product page, which is also each brand's official retail store. Nothing comes from Amazon. Nothing is AI-generated or stock.

Processing, the same for all seven: composited onto white, trimmed to the product, centred in a square with about 7% margin, resized to 600 × 600, saved as WebP (quality 82) with a JPEG fallback (quality 84). Files are in `website-next/img/buying-guide/`.

**Rights: the owner needs to review these before publishing.** None of these pages states a licence for the images. I did not find a press kit or media page with explicit terms for any of the seven. Product photos shown next to a retail link are a common and usually tolerated use, but none of these makers has granted it in writing. The safest route is a short email to each brand's marketing or press contact (or using the images that the Amazon Associates SiteStripe or API provides, under Amazon's licence). Until then, treat the terms below as "not stated".

## Initial draft notes (superseded by the emergency guide revision)

The guide has 21 Amazon links: 7 go with the named products above, and 14 are generic "Search Amazon for …" buttons (first aid manual, medicine organizer, bleach, fridge thermometer, camp stove, pressure canner, shut-off tool, tarp, power bank, CO alarm, generator, whistle, document bag, sleeping bag). Those 14 name no specific product, so there is no exact item to picture. They are left without images on purpose: a photo of one arbitrary brand would misrepresent a search, and stock photos were ruled out.

No named product is missing an image.
