No. 5

The full steps

Version 1.0 isn’t signed yet, so your computer asks you to confirm. You only do this once.

On a Mac

For Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer) with macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

  1. Open Lastbook-1.0.0-mac.dmg.
  2. Drag Lastbook to Applications. Then eject the Lastbook disk in the Finder sidebar.
  3. Leave Lastbook-Small.zip in Downloads and double-click it. Wait until it finishes. It makes a folder called LastbookData. Leave it there.
    If you already see a LastbookData folder in Downloads, leave it there and skip this step.
  4. Open Lastbook from Applications.

The first time, macOS warns that it can’t check Lastbook. That is expected. Don’t click Move to Trash.

macOS 15 Sequoia or newer
  1. Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to Security. Next to the line that says Lastbook was blocked, click Open Anyway.
  4. When asked, click Open Anyway (or Open), and use Touch ID or your Mac password.

If the button is gone, open Lastbook again and come back within an hour.

macOS 14 Sonoma
  1. Click Cancel.
  2. Open Applications in the Finder.
  3. Control-click Lastbook. Choose Open.
  4. Click Open.

You only do this once.

If your Mac asks to let Lastbook use your Downloads or Documents folder, click Allow. That is how it finds the library.

On Windows

For Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

  1. Right-click Lastbook-Small.zip in Downloads and choose Extract All.
  2. Click Extract. Keep the folder it suggests. Wait until it finishes, which can take several minutes.
  3. Double-click Lastbook-1.0.0-Setup.exe in Downloads.
  4. If Windows protected your PC appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  5. Click Install, then Open Lastbook.

Next time, open Lastbook from the Start menu.

Windows 11 only: Smart App Control may block Lastbook, because it isn’t signed yet. There is no way to allow just one app. If Windows says Smart App Control blocked Lastbook, open Windows Security > App & browser control > Smart App Control settings and choose Off. On an up-to-date Windows 11 you can turn it back on later. A signed version that doesn’t need this is coming.

Why the warning?

Apple and Microsoft expect app makers to register a digital signature. Lastbook doesn’t have one yet, so your computer shows a warning. A signed version comes later.

Lastbook never uses the internet.

It asks you. Choose the LastbookData folder. It is in Downloads, or on Windows in Downloads > Lastbook-Small.
