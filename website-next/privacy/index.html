Privacy and trust

Short version: Lastbook never uses the internet, and this site doesn’t track you.

The app never uses the internet

Everything is on your computer. Lastbook doesn’t check for updates, and it doesn’t send anything anywhere. There is no account to make.

What you save in Lastbook stays on your computer.

You can check: turn off Wi‑Fi and open Lastbook. Everything works the same.

This site doesn’t track you

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.
  • No fonts, scripts or pictures from other companies. Everything on these pages comes from lastbook.org.

The site is hosted by Vercel, and the download files are stored on Cloudflare. Like any web host, they may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses. We don’t add anything on top.

Not signed yet, and why

Apple and Microsoft expect app makers to register a digital signature. Lastbook doesn’t have one yet, so your computer shows a warning the first time you open it. A signed version comes later.
