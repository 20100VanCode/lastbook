How to open Lastbook

  1. Download and install the app for your Mac or Windows computer.
  2. Download a library (Small, Medium or All) separately. Unzip it in Downloads, then open Lastbook. Choose the LastbookData folder if asked.
  3. Read, search and watch with no internet. Local AI is an optional separate download; app integration is in progress.
